What If I'm Unsure?

If you are unsure what kind of job you want, think about your skills and hobbies or what you enjoy. Some opportunities, for example, if you like food, you can go work at a fast food restaurant. Or, if you enjoy computers and technology, you could work on tech.

Public Domain

What If I Just Want to Try?

It's common to feel unsure. If you just want to try out different things and jobs, you can try some jobs out or volunteer. Once you figure out what you want to do, you can apply to a real job above!

What About Just Volunteering?

If you are just wanting to help out your local community, you could also volunteer, which can help prepare you for a real job as well. If you want to volunteer, ask your school about it. They will most likely have things that need helpers to succeed.
