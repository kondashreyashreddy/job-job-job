The Problem

The job market in the big '25 is crashing and millions of people are unable to gain experience due to this issue. The majority of people who are being hit the most with this issue are teenagers. As most full-time jobs require prior job experience earlier in life, most of these teenagers in the modern age will be unable to succeed in life.

The Solution

Job Job Job, a non-profit organization, helps teenagers in high school find jobs in their desired work field. We partner with other establishments located near high schools that students can do during off periods. We also make sure that the jobs they do are flexible to make sure that the high schoolers in our programs can study and earn experience in their desired pathway. We also offer programs for middle school students that provide volunteering opportunities that can help with honors programs, like the National Junior Honors Society.
